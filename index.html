Top Selling Games

Call Of Duty

Call of Duty is a franchise of several multiplayer first-person shooter games published by Activision. The original game focused on the Second World War, but subsequent games have been set in different times and places, including futuristic worlds and outer space.

Guardians of the galaxy

In Marvel's Guardians of the Galaxy, the player assumes control of Peter Quill/Star-Lord from a third-person perspective to interact with the environment. During exploration or by choosing dialogue paths, he may unlock entries in the Galactic Compendium, or find Guardian Collectibles and different Outfits for the team.

Fortnite Cauchmars

Fortnite is a survival game where 100 players fight against each other in player versus player combat to be the last one standing. It is a fast-paced, action-packed game, not unlike The Hunger Games, where strategic thinking is a must in order to survive. There are an estimated 125 million players on Fortnite.

Consoles and Gaming Peripherals

PlayStation DualSense Edge™ Wireless Controller

Get an edge in gameplay with remappable buttons, tunable triggers and sticks, changeable stick caps, back buttons, and more. Built with high performance and personalization in mind, the controller also retains all the immersive features of the DualSense wireless controller.

Backbone One IOS XBOX Controller

The Backbone One iOS Xbox Controller is a sleek, portable gaming accessory designed to transform your iPhone into a fully functional gaming console. Officially licensed by Xbox, this controller offers an ergonomic design that provides a comfortable grip for extended gaming sessions.

playstation 5

The PS5 has a powerful CPU, which means a fast user interface, speedy load times, more sophisticated character and game behaviors, and just more stuff on screen at any given moment than the PS4 or Xbox One could produce.

Upcoming Games

grand theft auto vi

Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI), the latest installment in Rockstar Games' acclaimed series, is scheduled for release in late 2025 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Set in the expansive open world of Leonida—a fictional state inspired by Florida—the game prominently features Vice City, a reimagined version of Miami.

Dynasty warriors origins

Players assume the role of a nameless martial artist suffering from amnesia, navigating the complex landscape of the late Eastern Han period. This fresh narrative perspective offers a deeper immersion into the historical events of the Three Kingdoms era.

Dragon Age: The Veilguard

Players can select from various lineages—human, dwarf, elf, or qunari—and classes such as rogue, mage, or warrior. The game offers extensive customization options, including body size and features, independent of chosen voice or pronouns.
